\section{Product Overview}

\subsection{Introduction}

PeaPod for Microgreens is a custom aeroponic plant growth unit optimized for growing high-quality microgreens at minimal operating cost. The system is designed to be turn-key and easy to use, from germination to harvest. Full-stack automation and control systems ensure repeatability and reliability, enabling business owners to make surefire return on investment, making this product ideal for in-house restaurant production and small-scale commercial growing.
